# Sinced — Support

Need a hand with Sinced, found a bug, or have an idea? Email
**adityakoltedes@gmail.com** and include your iPhone model and iOS version if
something isn't working.

## Common questions

**My widget isn't updating.**
Open Sinced once. That refreshes every widget. If a widget shows "No event",
touch and hold it, choose **Edit Widget**, and pick an event.

**I'm not getting reminders.**
Check that notifications for Sinced are allowed in **Settings → Notifications →
Sinced**, and that reminders are turned on for the event in the app.

**Can I undo a reset?**
Yes, for two minutes after you reset: tap **Undo** in the app or on the widget.

**Where is my data stored? Can I move it to a new phone?**
Everything stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts or servers. Your events
move to a new phone with an iCloud or computer backup of your device.

**How do I delete my data?**
Delete individual events in the app, or delete the Sinced app to remove
everything.

See also the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).
