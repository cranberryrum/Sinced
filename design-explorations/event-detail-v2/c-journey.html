Direction C

Progress you can see

The page is about where you are on the way: a ring filling towards the goal, the milestones you've passed, and every run and day laid out.

Try

  • The ring fills on a spring as the page opens. The faint arc behind it is your best run, so you can see yourself pass it. Tap the number to change units.
  • Milestones you've passed are little cards in your theme; the next one has a soft ping.
  • Drag across Runs for each run's length, dates and note.
  • Last 12 weeks: every day you kept going, and every reset.
  • Hold to reset in the bottom bar, as in A.

Why

  • It shows the most of the history Sinced already keeps, so opening the app is worth it, not just glancing at the widget.
  • It's the most dense. A and B are calmer.
