Direction D

Bold, in the style of the GO Club app

Their language on Sinced's warm white: very large numbers with a small unit, capsule bars, a white panel at the bottom with the tabs built in, bright cards, and dark full-screen sheets for anything decisive.

Try

  • Home: swipe the counter cards and tap one. It grows into the event page. The "+" tab or the last card opens the new-counter sheet.
  • Event page: D / H / M / Y changes the unit, and the digits roll by place value. Drag across the bars: the big number rolls to that run's length.
  • The tabs in the white panel switch between runs, goal (a huge percent that turns from coral to yellow to green, with your card filling up as a bottle), the dot calendar and settings.
  • Reset opens the dark sheet: −/+ to backdate, an optional note, then hold the white pill.
  • Goal ›, and Delete in settings, use the same dark sheet.

What changed from their app

  • No blue: the background stays Sinced's warm white. Each event's own colour does the job their blue does.
  • Their water bottle becomes your card filling up towards the goal.
