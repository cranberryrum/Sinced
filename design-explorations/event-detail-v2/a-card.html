Direction A

The card is the page

The event's own card, the one on the home grid and the widget, grows up into the whole top of the page. Everything else sits quietly below it on white cards.

Try

  • Tap the number to cycle days, hours, months and years. It morphs like Torph, and the unit you land on is what the widget shows.
  • Hold to reset: a filled copy wipes across under your finger, with buzzes that get stronger as you hold. Let go early and it snaps back. Accidental resets are off the table.
  • Drag across "Every run": each bar is a run, ticking as you pass it, with its note.
  • Remind me, Delete and Edit open the add card's floating sheet.
  • Swap themes at the top. The whole page takes on the card's colour.

Why

  • It's continuous with home: the card you tapped is the card you land on.
  • One big number, one goal, one action, then the history.
