    Direction B

    The page reads like the add card

    The add card is a sentence you fill in. The event page is the same sentence, finished and still editable. There's no Edit mode: every bold word is a button.

    Try

    • "23 days" cycles days, hours, months and years with a morph.
    • Tap the name, the goal, the reminder or the card thumbnail: each opens one small editor in the floating card, grown out of the word you tapped.
    • Reset grows the same card out of the button: "Reset no caffeine, from now, with a note". Tap "now" to step back in time. Confirm, and the whole paragraph rewrites itself while the old run drops into the story.
    • The story is every run, with its note and a bar against your best.

    Why

    • It's the most "Sinced" of the three: the add card and the event page share one language.
    • Words carry the facts ("5 days past your old best") instead of labels and boxes.
