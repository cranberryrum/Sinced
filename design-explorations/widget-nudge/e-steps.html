Direction E

Steps inline

Starts small, like today's card. Tap it and it opens in place into a 20-second walkthrough that plays the three steps on a tiny Home Screen, so nobody has to leave Home or read a guide.

What changes

  • No separate guide sheet. The steps live in the card. Progress bars like stories; tap the preview to skip ahead.
  • Each step shows the gesture. Icons jiggle after the long press, the Edit button gets tapped, "Sinced" gets typed into the widget search.
  • Small when closed, so it's fine to keep it around until a widget is added.

Motion

  • Opens with a 400ms height reveal (the one place height is fair game: it's an accordion), content fades in after 100ms.
  • Steps crossfade at 250ms; progress bars fill linearly over each step's 2.6s, since they're time, not UI.
  • Reduced motion: icons don't jiggle, the finger and sheet just appear.
