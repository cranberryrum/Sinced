Lock Screen

Sells the moment people actually check: picking up the phone. A little Lock Screen wakes up inside the nudge and the counter appears under the clock.

What changes

  • The payoff, not the chore. Instead of "add a widget", it shows the count waiting for you every time you glance at the time.
  • Lock Screen first. It's the quickest widget to add and the one seen most; the guide can still offer the Home Screen sizes.
  • Copy in Sinced's voice: "See No sugar every time you check the time."

Motion

  • Slot opens like A. Then the screen "wakes": the dark overlay lifts over 500ms, ease-out.
  • The two widgets rise 8px and fade in, 60ms apart, 400ms ease-out; the ring fills to the first milestone.
  • Reduced motion: fades only.
