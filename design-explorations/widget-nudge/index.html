What today's card gets wrong

  • Generic: "Add it to your Home Screen" never names the counter.
  • Tells instead of shows: a thumbnail and two lines don't explain what a widget will look like or how to add one.
  • Appears with no relationship to the card you just made.
  • Only one way out (the ×), and no sense of how long adding takes.

What every direction keeps

  • Shows once, after the first counter, and goes away for good once a widget is added.
  • Leaves the way it came, quicker than it arrived.
  • Transform and opacity only (plus one accordion height in E), strong ease-out, under 450ms for UI.
  • Reduced motion keeps the fades and drops the movement.
