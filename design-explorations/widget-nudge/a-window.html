Direction A

Window

The nudge shows a little Home Screen with an empty widget spot, and the counter you just made flies from the grid into it. It explains the whole idea in one move, before any words.

What changes

  • Show, don't tell. The card that just appeared travels into the preview, so the link between "this counter" and "a widget" is obvious.
  • Named after the counter. "Keep No sugar in sight" instead of a generic line.
  • Two clear choices. "Show me how" opens the guide; "Later" folds the card away the way it came.
  • Tap the preview to play the flight again.

Motion

  • Nudge: slot opens 420ms, card rises 8px and fades in, strong ease-out.
  • Flight: a copy of the counter moves and shrinks into the slot, 700ms strong ease-in-out (it's moving on screen), then the widget settles in.
  • Reduced motion: no flight, the widget just fades into its spot.
