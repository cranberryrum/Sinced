Direction B

Callout

No banner pushing the grid down. A callout grows straight out of the counter you just made, the way a tip points at the thing it's about, with every widget size you can add previewed inside it.

What changes

  • Anchored to the counter. It grows from under the new card, so it's clearly about that card.
  • Pick a size first. Small, Medium and Lock Screen preview live, cycling on their own until you touch one. The guide then opens at that size.
  • Nothing moves. The grid stays where it was; the callout floats over the empty space below it.

Motion

  • The counter gets one ring pulse, then the callout scales from 0.95 at the arrow, 220ms strong ease-out. Leaves at 150ms.
  • Sizes crossfade with a 0.96 scale, 280ms; the segmented thumb slides with the same curve.
  • Auto-cycle stops for good on the first tap.
