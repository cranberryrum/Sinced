Variant B

Insights dashboard

This version treats the page as a progress report. It answers "how am I doing?" with data. Reset history is shown as a chart of every streak, milestones sit on a ladder, and the unit picker moves next to the widget preview, since that's what it controls.

Information architecture

  1. Header: identityTheme thumbnail, title, tracking-since date and streak number. Share and edit sit top-left, Done top-right.
  2. Now: live clock and resetA D : H : M : S clock that ticks every second. Reset sits inside the card for the thing it resets. A personal-best badge shows when earned.
  3. Performance: four statsLongest, average, resets, and time tracked. All of it comes from history and needs no new data.
  4. Journey: milestones and streak chartA 1 → 7 → 30 → 90 → 365 ladder. Bars show every streak against the average; tap a bar to read its reset note.
  5. Surfaces: widget and remindersA live widget preview with the unit picker (widgetTimeUnit). Reminders expand inline, with no extra sheet.
  6. ManageDelete sits at the very bottom, away from Reset.

Trade-offs

Why pick it

  • Makes reset history motivating instead of a plain log
  • The unit picker finally explains itself (it's for the widget)
  • Reminders are configured in place, so there's no extra sheet
  • Adding goals or insights later is easy

Watch out for

  • The densest variant, and the most to build
  • Brand and theme carry less feeling here
  • New events with 0 resets need empty states for the chart and stats

Try: tap the bars in the chart, change "Count in" to update the widget, turn reminders off and on, and reset to watch a new bar appear.
