Variant A

Immersive card

The home card grows into the page. Opening an event feels like picking up the card itself. The time and theme fill the top, and a sheet underneath holds everything else. Reset and Share stay pinned to the bottom.

Information architecture

  1. Hero: identity and live timeUnit switch, big mono counter, "Since …", start-date badge, live d/h/m/s. The theme background is kept from the home card.
  2. ProgressThe next milestone with a bar between the previous and next marks. A "personal best" callout appears when the current streak beats the longest one.
  3. SettingsReminders (inline toggle), card theme and widget as one inset list. Each row opens its own sheet.
  4. HistoryA vertical timeline of past streaks, with how long each lasted and the reset note.
  5. Pinned: Reset and ShareThe dock hides while you scroll down and comes back when you scroll up. Delete and edit move into the ••• menu.

Trade-offs

Why pick it

  • Continuous with the home card, so a zoom/matched-geometry transition works naturally
  • The most emotional of the three; the theme carries the page
  • The one main action is always within thumb reach
  • Delete moves off the main surface

Watch out for

  • The hero takes about 55% of the first screen
  • Not much room for stats or insights later
  • Custom photo themes need a scrim for legibility

Try: switch units on the card, scroll the sheet up (the dock hides), open ••• to see where edit and delete live, and tap Reset timer for the reset sheet. Change the card theme in the top bar.
