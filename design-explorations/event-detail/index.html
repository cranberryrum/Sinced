Three directions for the event detail page

All three show the same event (streak #5 of "No smoking", a personal best at 23 days) and all use the fields in SinceEvent: time unit, milestones, reset history with notes, reminders and card theme. What changes is the information architecture: what comes first, where actions live, and how history is told.

A starting opinion

C's structure, with A's hero feel. Tabs keep the motivating view clean as features get added, and chapters make reset history encouraging instead of a list of failures.

B's streak chart and stats could fit into the Journey tab later without changing the IA. Test hold-to-reset with a few users before committing; a confirm sheet is the safe fallback.
