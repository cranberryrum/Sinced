The add sheet as a sentence

One floating card that grows out of the + tile. Each bold word opens its own editor inside the same card, and saving turns the card into your new event.

  1. Tap + to open the sheet.
  2. Tap any bold word: the name, the date, the goal or the theme.
  3. Pick a name idea, or type on your keyboard (Return finishes).
  4. Drag the goal wheel or scroll on it.
  5. Tap Save to watch the sheet become a card. Drag down or tap outside to close.
