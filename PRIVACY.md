# Sinced — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 13, 2026_

Sinced ("the app", "we", "us") is a small app for keeping track of the time since
moments that matter to you. This policy explains what the app stores, why, and the
choices you have. We've kept it plain and honest.

## The short version

- **Everything stays on your device.** Sinced has no accounts, no servers and no
  cloud sync. We never see your events.
- **We don't track you, run ads, or sell your data.** There are no analytics or
  advertising SDKs in the app.
- **The app doesn't connect to the internet.** The only time it opens a web page is
  when you tap a link, like this policy or the developer's website.

## What the app stores

All of this is saved only on your iPhone, in storage shared between the app and its
Home Screen widget:

- **Your events.** The name of each event, when it started, its card theme, how it
  displays on the widget, and any reset history and notes you add.
- **Photos you choose for a card.** If you pick a custom photo, a small, compressed
  copy is saved with that event. You choose a specific photo through Apple's system
  picker, which does not give the app access to your whole photo library.
- **Your name**, if you enter one, and your app preferences (like whether
  reminders are turned on).

## Reminders

If you turn on reminders, Sinced schedules notifications **on your device** using
iOS's local notifications. Nothing is sent to us or to any other server.

## Saving and sharing cards

- **Save to Photos.** If you save a card, Sinced asks for permission to **add** images
  to your photo library. It cannot read your existing photos.
- **Share.** If you share a card, the image goes only to the app or person you choose
  in the iOS share sheet.

## Who can see your data

Only you, on your device. Your data is not shared with us or any third party. If you
use iCloud or iTunes device backups, your data is included in those backups under
Apple's terms, as with any app.

## Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it:

- Delete an individual event from inside the app.
- Delete everything by deleting the Sinced app from your iPhone.

## Children

Sinced is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal
information from them. In fact, it doesn't collect personal information from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, the "last updated" date
above will change.
